Research Highlights

Mapping the prokaryotic noncoding sequence space with Evo-PIN

Much of the noncoding DNA in prokaryotic genomes is still uncharted. I built COMPASS, a pipeline that uses a fine-tuned DNA language model to map this sequence space. It rediscovered many recently found noncoding systems and turned up thousands of new ones that no one has characterized yet.

bioRxiv (2026) Website

Evo 2: a language model trained on DNA

I worked on Evo 2, an open source language model that learned to read DNA from 9 trillion base pairs spanning the tree of life. Without any task-specific supervision, we showed it can predict the effects of genetic mutations, including clinically significant variants of BRCA1.

Nature (2026) GitHub

E. coli Whole-Cell Modeling

I built and extended a whole-cell model of E. coli that brings thousands of biochemical reactions, from transcription and translation to metabolism and DNA replication, into a single genome-scale simulation. We used it to cross-check diverse experimental datasets against each other and to ask how operon structure shapes gene expression and cellular physiology.

Cell Systems (2024) Science (2020) GitHub
